The fit, in one line

Across the 119 protocols that already trade, market cap grows like TVL^0.4721, not in proportion to TVL: the fitted line is log10(mcap) = 3.3823 + 0.4721 · log10(TVL). Each board row then adds half the log of its three round-2 multipliers — growth, maturity and chains — so a fast-growing, long-running protocol on several chains scores above the size line, and a shrinking young one scores below it. Every coefficient is refit from this snapshot on every run; the flat ×0.246 multiple is shown only as the baseline it replaces.

  • Elasticity 0.4721: value scales sub-linearly with size — a flat multiple overprices big protocols.
  • Growth, age and chains are scored, at half strength: the blind test weakens round 2's multipliers but does not zero them — growth clipped to [−50, +100]% and weighted 0.6, maturity 0.65–1.00 over 1460 days, chains +2% per extra chain up to +10%.
  • One-per-ticker comps: the live set is deduped by ticker (0 symbol rows and 0 CoinGecko rows dropped), so versions of one token never count twice.
  • Rank ranges come from 200 bootstrap refits of the shipped model; the fitted line alone cannot move a rank, so the ranges are narrow at the top and wider mid-board.
  • Coverage: all 40 watchlist slugs rank, plus 138 pre-token protocols below the $50M floor.

Repeated 5-fold (seeds 0–2, TVL-desc order, seeded shuffle): the shipped model scores 0.9183; the flat baseline 0.9806; the fitted line alone 0.9133. Walk-forward (train on the past, test on the future): at the 60% cut the flat baseline scores 1.11, the fitted line 0.95 and the shipped model 0.97; at the 80% cut 1.30, 1.14 and 1.18 — the most recent listings are the hardest to price, and the shipped model still beats the flat baseline on both cuts. Where the flat multiple misses worst — protocols over $1B TVL — its mean error is −1.12 dex against −0.08 dex for the shipped model.

Honest limits: the fitted elasticity is measured on live protocols and may not transfer to pre-token ones; the board is a ranking, not a price target; comps are the nearest live protocols by size, not endorsements; a launch's real value also depends on the token's supply, unlocks and market timing, none of which this snapshot carries.
