      What this cannot say

      • Best R² out-of-sample is about +0.13: roughly 87% of log market-cap variance is not explained by any snapshot field. Implied values are order-of-magnitude estimates; the product is the ranking plus its uncertainty band, not the dollar figure.
      • The live set is a selected sample: protocols that have launched. Pre-token protocols may differ systematically, and no snapshot field corrects for that.
      • One snapshot, one date. No time series exists in the input, so no trend, seasonality or regime statement is possible.
      • Growth, age and chain count are displayed as tested-and-dropped, not as scorers: the blind test rules them out as value drivers on this snapshot.
