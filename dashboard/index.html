How the figure is made

Every implied value is computed from the snapshot's own figures — nothing is looked up, nothing is hardcoded by name. The size leg is a power law fitted on the 119 protocols that already record a token:

log10(market cap) = 3.3823 + 0.4721 · log10(TVL)

Then three legs the blind test kept, each at half strength — the damping is the blind test's verdict, not a guess:

  • Sector. A shrunk median residual per category (k = 20, capped at ±0.30 dex). Liquid Staking and Lending sit below the size line, RWA and Canonical Bridge above it. The mean variant loses on the blind test and raw category multiples lose badly — neither is used.
  • Momentum. Seven-day TVL change, clipped to −50/+100%, weight 0.6, at half strength. A spike predicts a lower multiple — incentive-driven deposits mean-revert — so it is damped, never dropped.
  • Age & chains. Maturity 0.65 → 1.00 over 1,460 days (unknown age = 365 days), +2% per extra chain to +10%, at half strength.

The farm verdict is arithmetic, not a slogan. For a $1,000 deposit farmed 60 days: slice = 10% of the implied value × ($1,000 / TVL) × dollar-days, where dollar-days = 60 / (60 + age/2); net = slice − $5 fees − $6.58 of T-bill yield forgone (4% × $1,000 × 60/365). FARM when the net is positive, THIN when it loses less than $10, else SKIP. Break-even deposit and break-even drop share are shown per row; the what-if controls below recompute every verdict live.

Launch odds are shown, not multiplied in. The launch share is the cross-sectional share of protocols that record a token, by age and by category. That is a share of a cross-section, not a forward hazard: a protocol that has run a days without launching has survived a days, so the share conditions on survival. One snapshot cannot calibrate a forward forecast, and this board does not pretend it can.

The whole 758, accounted for

The watchlist is a floor, never a ceiling. This board ranks every standalone pre-token protocol — including the ones above the watchlist's $50M floor, which the last round left out entirely. The rest of the snapshot is set aside for a stated reason each:

  • 201 ranked — every standalone pre-token protocol (40 watchlist + the rest, floor or not).
  • 128 record a token — they already launched; the radar is for what has not.
  • 199 set aside by category — Liquid Staking 69, Bridge 49, CEX 47, Canonical Bridge 24, Liquid Restaking 7, Restaked BTC 3.
  • 52 versioned names — a name ending in V2, v2, 2.0, V3, v3 or 3.0 is a version of a protocol, not a new one.
  • 6 wrapped / staked names — a wrapped or staked product of a token that already trades.
  • 0 exchange names — a protocol named after an exchange.
  • 172 kin of a token — a gecko_id or symbol is recorded but no market cap, so no live row to learn from.

What this can and cannot say

  • The live set is a selected sample: protocols that have launched. Pre-token protocols may differ systematically — survivorship. The board's median age is far younger than the live set's, and Risk Curators has 33 board rows against one live row. Thin-category comps are labelled cross-category where the live set has fewer than 3 rows in the category.
  • Best R² out of sample is 0.113: most of log market-cap variance is not explained by any snapshot field. That is why every row carries a rank range (p10–p90 from 200 bootstrap refits) — the range is the honest statement of precision.
  • One snapshot, one date (14 Nov 2026). No time series exists in the input, so no trend, seasonality or regime statement is possible.
  • Growth, age and chains are scored at half-damped weights because the blind test weakens them — the damping is the blind test's verdict, never a claim that they were dropped.
  • Values are the entry's own claims, never make.fun's. A protocol that records no token may still have one its DefiLlama record does not carry.
